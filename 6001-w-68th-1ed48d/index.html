Before Closing Day

  • Lock in homeowners insurance and send the policy to your lender
  • Don't open new credit, change jobs, or make big purchases until after closing
  • Answer lender requests quickly. Underwriting moves at the speed of your inbox
  • Review your closing disclosure as soon as it arrives and send me any questions
  • Never act on wiring instructions sent by email. Call Platinum Title at 913-491-5001 to confirm before sending a dime
  • Bring a photo ID to closing
  • Plan the move for after you have keys, not before
